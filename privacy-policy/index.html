Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24th August 2025

At The PC Radar, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This includes information such as pages visited, time spent on the site, and general device/browser details.
  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display ads. AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads based on your interests and activity.
  • Firebase: We use Firebase to collect site interaction data. This includes actions like when a user votes on an article.
  • Local Storage: We use your browser's local storage to save your preferences, such as recording your vote on an article.
  • Session Storage: We use your browser's session storage to save data from the server so that loading is faster on pages like the home feed.

How We Use Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Improve the content and usability of our site.
  • Provide relevant ads and offers.
  • Understand which features are useful to our visitors.
  • Monitor site performance and fix any technical issues.

Cookies and Tracking

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies through Google Analytics and AdSense. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but some features of the site may not function properly without them.

Third-Party Services

Google Analytics, Google AdSense, and Firebase are third-party services that may collect, store, and process data according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies to learn more about how your data is handled.

Your Choices

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also use browser tools or extensions to block tracking and cookies if you prefer.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.
